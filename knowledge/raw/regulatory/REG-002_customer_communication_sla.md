# REG-002: Customer communication service levels (internal summary)

This is the bank's internal communication standard for the fictional bank used in this project. It is synthetic.

## 1. Service levels

| Severity | First customer-facing status | Updates | Resolution notice |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Within 30 minutes of the page | Every 60 minutes | Within 1 hour of recovery |
| S2 | Within 60 minutes | Every 2 hours | Within 2 hours of recovery |
| S3, S4 | Only if customers ask | Not required | Not required |

## 2. Channels

Customer-facing status goes to the app's status banner, the website's service status page, and the call-centre script. Social media replies point to the status page.

## 3. Content standards

- Plain language: describe what customers cannot do, not the technology.
- No blame on staff, partners or suppliers.
- Only confirmed facts and figures.
- Tell customers whether they need to do anything, for example "you do not need to retry the payment".
- For incidents affecting funds: say that any incorrect charge will be refunded automatically and when.

## 4. Approval

The incident manager approves every customer-facing message for S1 and S2 incidents. Messages about funds also need the compliance officer's approval.
